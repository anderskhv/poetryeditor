## Finding Broken Links

### Manual Method
1. Visit resource pages from list above
2. Click each external link
3. Note any 404s or redirects to dead domains
4. Reach out with replacement suggestion

### Automated Tools (Free Tiers)
- **Ahrefs Broken Link Checker** - Check competitor backlinks for 404s
- **Dead Link Checker** - Scan pages for broken outbound links
- **Check My Links** - Chrome extension for quick scanning

### High-Probability Targets
These types of links often go dead:
- Links to RhymeZone (sometimes changes structure)
- Links to old poetry databases
- Links to educational tools that shut down
- Links to personal poetry blogs

## Notes

- Always personalize emails (reference specific content on their site)
- Follow up once after 1 week if no response
- Don't spam - quality over quantity
- Track everything in spreadsheet
- Expect ~10-15% response rate, ~5% conversion to actual links
